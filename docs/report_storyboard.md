# Experimental report editorial plan

Revised 10 October 2026. Complete English and Chinese versions use identical data, definitions and section order. The report has no appendix and does not repeat the companion theory note's proofs.

The argument separates three questions: whether the robust optimization is solved, which coordinates it chooses, and how those coordinates perform on independent observations. The frozen 1,640-record study answers the latter questions for the earlier implementation. A separate, versioned development and confirmation study tests the revised next-subset search. Neither supports nor timings are transferred between versions.

1. **Design and implementation.** Explain the four synthetic mechanisms, two UCI datasets, independent selection/fitting/testing splits, fixed parameters, six budget-matched selectors, baseline adaptations and metric definitions. Two compact settings tables and one selector table give the information needed to reproduce the experiment.
2. **Revised-search validation.** Keep development comparisons separate by code snapshot. Preserve unsuccessful ranking rules, extra-cost dual-face selection and any fraction-master failures. After a rule is locked, report the separate confirmation cases and paired evaluation/runtime plots. Counts of conditions and distinct generated datasets are both stated.
3. **Frozen computation.** A runtime/solved-fraction figure carries scaling; one D=12 table records the principal comparison. Enumeration agreement and the earlier complete-fraction supplement remain explicit.
4. **Frozen statistical utility.** Power and null rejection share a figure; signal recall and common-SVM error share a second figure. Four-row tables distinguish Wang's native signed statistic from support-only RBF evaluation and fitted empirical bounds from independent error. Compact radius and real-data tables retain unfavorable results.
5. **Recorded outcomes.** One suite-level inventory retains all failures and limits. Explain the twelve original replays, later failures, charged time, actual solver backend and reproducibility files. Detailed secondary metrics and individual traces remain in canonical CSV/JSON rather than repeated report tables.

This selection follows the experiment sections actually read in Wang et al., *Variable Selection for Kernel Two-Sample Tests* (pp. 16–23), *Robust Hypothesis Testing with Wasserstein Uncertainty Sets* (pp. 19–21), the Sinkhorn robust-testing paper (pp. 11–13, 20–22), and the generative Sinkhorn robust-testing paper (pp. 16–24 and Appendix D). The first separates computation, power and recovery; the robust-testing papers motivate independent classification error, radius sensitivity and explicit runtime limits. Their tasks and published tables are not claimed to be reproduced literally.

Style: black 11pt article, one-inch margins, Times New Roman/Cambria Math and readable Chinese text. Figures use matched English/Chinese axes, restrained colors, conventional captions and intervals appropriate to the actual independent unit. Aim for a concise report without reducing type size to force a page count. Every observed result is generated from audited records; paired builds and visual review precede publication.
